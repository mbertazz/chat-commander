Chat Commander

Connect Chat Commander one step at a time. After restarting, begin by unlocking your saved key.

1 Prepare your connection key

This key lets the extension receive requests from your ChatGPT app. You need an existing private OpenAI tunnel and its dedicated runtime key. Installing the extension does not create them.

Follow the OpenAI guide to create a tunnel and key

Already saved your key? Unlock it here with your local password. This is not your ChatGPT password and is not sent to OpenAI.

Set up or replace the key

You need an OpenAI tunnel and the Chat Commander app configured in your ChatGPT. Use a dedicated runtime key, not an Admin key.

The password unlocks the key after a restart and is not saved by the extension. If you forget it, enter the API key again. Encryption protects the saved file, not a compromised browser or computer.

    2 Connect your ChatGPT tab 
    

    
Complete step 1 first. Then you can choose and connect a tab.

    
Open ChatGPT, sign in to your personal account and wait for the page to load. Return here and choose the tab to connect: the extension uses it to read chats and verify requested changes.

    
    
The browser will ask for access to chatgpt.com. The connection result appears directly below the button. The connected tab is protected from moves and deletion.

    
    
    
    

    

    
    
After restarting: unlock your key, open an authenticated ChatGPT tab and wait to reconnect to the same account. If it does not reconnect, return to step 2.

    

  

  

    3 Use Chat Commander in ChatGPT 
    

    
Complete steps 1 and 2 first. Test the app connection inside ChatGPT, rather than saving the key again.

    

      
Complete ChatGPT setup guide

      
Connect the Chat Commander app to your tunnel once in ChatGPT settings. After a tool update, use Manage → Refresh tools. If the app is already connected and up to date, do not recreate it.

      

    

    
Then send this message in your ChatGPT conversation. The list and any errors will appear in the chat: that is where you verify the full connection.

    
Use Chat Commander to list my 3 most recent chats. Do not modify anything.

    

    

  

  

    Manage connection
    
Locking stops the tunnel and requires the password again. Forgetting also removes the saved encrypted key: you will need to enter the runtime key again.
